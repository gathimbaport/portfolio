Questions

Before you get in touch.

A few common questions about working with a quantity surveyor.

When should a quantity surveyor be brought in?

As early as possible. Cost advice has the greatest influence while the design is still developing, and early cost planning helps avoid expensive redesign later.

What information do you need to get started?

Whatever exists at the time: the brief, drawings, specifications and the budget range. The available information shapes the type of estimate that can be prepared.

Do you work on smaller projects such as fitouts and renovations?

Yes. Work covers residential development,Industrial development ,commercial development ,fitout and renovation projects.
